WHAT WE DO

Core Embroidery & Digitizing Services.

Helping embroidery businesses, apparel brands, and professionals bring their artwork to life through professional embroidery digitizing.

Logo & Custom Digitizing

Clean, production-ready embroidery digitizing tailored for brand logos and custom designs.

3D Puff & Cap Digitizing

Specialized stitch planning and density control for structured caps and raised 3D puff embroidery.

Jacket & Hoodie Digitizing

Fabric-specific underlay and stitch optimization for heavy outerwear, jackets, and hoodies.

Small Lettering & Appliqué

Crisp, readable micro-lettering and clean appliqué borders engineered for smooth stitching.

Kids & Baby Designs

Soft, low-density, and delicate embroidery digitizing crafted specifically for kids and baby apparel.

Pet Portrait & Photo Digitizing

Detailed color blending and stitch mapping to turn portraits and photos into realistic embroidery.

DTF Printing Files

High-resolution, transparent PNG files prepped and optimized for vibrant DTF printing.

Screen Printing Artwork

Clean, color-separated, print-ready artwork designed for sharp screen printing results.

Vector Art & File Conversion

Precision vector tracing and production-ready file conversions in AI, EPS, SVG, and PDF formats.

With a focus on stitch quality, proper density, underlay, and fabric-specific techniques, I deliver reliable, production-ready embroidery files.

Looking for a dependable embroidery digitizing partner? Feel free to reach out for custom projects and digitizing inquiries.
